# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 25, 2026  
**Applicability:** Mind Dialogue App (Pakistan)

Welcome to Mind Dialogue. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. Because our application deals with sensitive emotional and mental health data, security and privacy are our highest priorities.

### 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Information:** When you create an account, we may collect basic details such as your email address and profile name.
- **Encrypted Conversations:** The messages you exchange with your AI companions are processed to generate responses. These messages are stored using **zero-knowledge encryption**. 
- **Wellness Data:** We track your mood logs, session themes, and engagement streaks to provide you with insights into your emotional well-being.

### 2. How We Use Your Data
- To provide, operate, and maintain the Mind Dialogue application.
- To generate intelligent, context-aware AI therapy responses.
- To analyze mood trends and provide you with personal wellness summaries.

### 3. Data Encryption and Privacy (Zero-Knowledge)
Mind Dialogue is built with a privacy-first architecture. Your chat messages are encrypted. Our backend servers store only the ciphertexts (scrambled data) and do not have the keys to read your plaintext conversations. 

### 4. Third-Party Services
We utilize third-party Large Language Models (LLMs), such as Groq, to power our AI personas. Data sent to these providers is strictly used for real-time response generation and is **never** used by these third parties to train their AI models. 

### 5. Data Retention and Erasure
You have complete control over your data. You can delete individual chat sessions or request a full account erasure through the app. Upon requesting account deletion, all your encrypted messages, mood logs, and personal data will be permanently wiped from our databases.

### 6. Compliance and Jurisdiction
This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with general data protection principles and the applicable laws of the **Islamic Republic of Pakistan**. 

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and providing a notice within the app.

*If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact our support team.*
